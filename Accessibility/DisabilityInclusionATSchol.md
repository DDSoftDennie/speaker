# Disability Inclusion and IT workshops at SCHOL

Is learning a privilege for the smart and the privileged? Should we separate the intellectuals from the illiterates? Personally, I say a big NO!

Since the birth of SCHOL - an experience shop in Ostend (2024) - I 've been asked to give monthly IT-workshops for everyone. With everyone I mean everyone on the IQ and ability spectrum.  Each monthly session has attendees with and without disabilities highly and lowly educated people, young and less young people.

Today I learn you how to design workshops for different attendees. What language you can use and how you can use metaphors for difficult concepts. For example let's look how to teach concepts like the cloud, AI, data and 2FA.

We can even take this a step further and look how to teach people with an intellectual disability with reading difficulties on how to use a smartphone, facebook and keeping online contact with friends, communities and family.
