# Moving from Azure IaaS to PaaS, my own story

Between 2014 - 2019 I was working with data, specific open data. DDSoft provided its own data client for its applications for people with disabilities. At that time it was an open data platform but we saw the future with a closed data possibility. I made the first version of our data platform in Azure in 2014. It was build in Infrastructure as a Service. In 2018 I made the switch to Platform as a Service. This is a win-win situation. In this talk I tell you about the good, the bad and the ugly of IaaS and PaaS. The steps I had to take to transfer to PaaS. I talk also of all the goodness the PaaS data platform has.
